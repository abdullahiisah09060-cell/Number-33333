Standard Operating Procedure

Bureau of the Fiscal Service Protocol

01

File Initialization

Submit your primary application for eligibility tier assessment. System algorithms determine initial grant capacity based on tax ID history.

02

Identity Audit (KYC)

Verification of identity through the secure federal ID-Vault. This step is mandatory to prevent fraudulent disbursement of public funds.

03

Tax Seal Compliance

Clear federal tax requirements to authorize large-sum payout nodes. Administrative fees or tax stamps may be required for cross-state clearance.

04

Node Disbursement

Final bank synchronization and release of grant funds. Assets are cleared via the Federal Reserve Ledger into your verified account.
