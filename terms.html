Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for consideration under the 2025 Federal Grant Cycle, applicants must verify the following criteria through the portal's identity and business audit systems:

  • The applicant business must be a for-profit or non-profit entity registered and physically operating within the 50 United States or U.S. territories.
  • The business must meet the SBA size standards as defined by NAICS codes (typically fewer than 500 employees).
  • Applicants must provide a valid Employer Identification Number (EIN) issued by the IRS.
  • The primary account holder must be a U.S. Citizen or Legal Permanent Resident aged 18 years or older.
  • The business must not have any delinquent federal debt or active federal debarment records.

Account Registration and Security

Applicants are granted a single, non-transferable account for the management of grant requests. You are responsible for the security of your credentials, including your multi-factor authentication (MFA) devices and your 4-digit Transaction PIN.

The SBA will never ask for your password or PIN via email or phone. Any unauthorized access resulting from the sharing of credentials is the sole responsibility of the user. We reserve the right to suspend any account suspected of compromised security or fraudulent activity.

Grant Application Process

Submitting an application via Form 424-B constitutes a formal request for federal funds. Each submission undergoes a three-stage audit: Automated Intake, Case Officer Review, and Board Authorization. The SBA reserves the right to request supplemental evidence, including tax transcripts, bank statements, or site inspection photos, at any time.

"Submission of an application is an expression of interest and does not guarantee the awarding of any federal funds or the obligation of the U.S. Treasury."

Deposit and Fee Requirements

Certain federal programs for large-scale disbursements (>$50,000) require a Tax Clearance Certificate fee under IRS Regulation §6325. This fee is used to process federal lien checks and generate the electronic certificate required for the Treasury's disbursement system.

All fees must be settled through the authorized payment channels within the portal. Any payments made to external, non-portal addresses are not recognized by the SBA and will result in processing delays or application cancellation.

Grant Disbursement Process

Approved funds are credited to the Recipient's secure Treasury Wallet within the portal. This wallet acts as a controlled ledger for federal oversight. Funds remain under federal jurisdiction until a successful withdrawal is authorized and completed to the Recipient's external business account.

Withdrawal Policy

Withdrawals from the Treasury Wallet are subject to 24-hour cooling periods and manual review for anti-money laundering (AML) compliance. Users must verify the destination account (Routing/Account or Wallet Address). Federal wires and blockchain settlements are irreversible once broadcast. The SBA is not liable for funds lost due to incorrect destination data provided by the user.

User Conduct and Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited and will result in permanent federal debarment and referral to the Department of Justice:

  • Submission of forged or digitally altered identity documents.
  • Use of automated scripts, bots, or scrapers to access the portal.
  • Providing false information regarding business revenue or employee counts.
  • Attempting to circumvent the Tax Clearance requirements.

Privacy and Data Protection

Data submitted to the portal is protected under the Privacy Act of 1974. We employ 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Information is shared only with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and the Department of the Treasury for the sole purpose of disbursement verification.
