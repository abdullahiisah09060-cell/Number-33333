1.0 AUTHORIZATION & DISCLOSURE

By accessing this portal, the applicant provides express consent to the Small Business Administration (SBA) to perform cross-agency data validation. This includes verification of PII with the IRS and DHS.

2.5 DISBURSEMENT PROTOCOLS

Funding is subject to final manual review by an SBA Case Officer. Approved funds are disbursed via ACH/Wire Transfer only to verified financial accounts in the applicant's legal name.

4.0 DATA SOVEREIGNTY

User data is protected under FIPS 140-2 compliance standards. All personally identifiable information (PII) is encrypted using 256-bit AES protocols and stored in secure federal nodes.

7.2 FRAUD & PENALTIES

Any attempt to provide fraudulent information is a federal offense. Violations result in immediate account termination and referral to the OIG for prosecution under 18 U.S.C. § 1001.
